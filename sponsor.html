Help us put on the first hack event.

An ongoing series where builders come together to explore real problems, experiment with ideas and build working solutions. Every hack is documented in a public record. We're looking for support to help make these events happen.

What we need.

You can help in kind, by providing the item or service itself, or with a contribution toward its cost.

Venue

A space for a small group of up to 10 people for a full session, with seating, power and reliable internet.

How to help: offer a space free or at a discount, or contribute toward the cost of hiring one.

Refreshments

Food and drinks to keep everyone going through the session.

How to help: supply or sponsor the refreshments, or contribute toward the cost.

Merch

Branded items for participants, such as t-shirts, stickers and notebooks carrying the drumsticks[hack] mark.

How to help: cover the printing, supply the items, or contribute toward the cost.

Contribute.

To give toward any of the above, please send your contribution to the account below. Kindly put your name or organisation in the reference, and let us know what purpose the contribution is toward. This will help us to acknowledge your generosity and utilise the contribution appropriately.
